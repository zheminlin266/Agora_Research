Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results](https://investor.agora.io/news-releases/news-release-details/agora-inc-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results), published November 16, 2026. Cross-checked against the [company's GlobeNewswire release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/16/3404189/0/en/agora-inc-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results.html).

The release is unaudited. Income and cash-flow values below use the **three months ended September 30, 2026**, not the nine-month cumulative column. Balance-sheet values are as of September 30, 2026. Historical table rows are unchanged.

- Gross margin: **63.4%**. Check: 26,696 / 42,097 × 100 = 63.4155%, rounding to 63.4%.
- Stock-based compensation: 2 (cost of revenue) + 617 (R&D) + 137 (sales and marketing) + 562 (G&A) = 1,318. This also agrees with the cash-flow statement.
- Operating result: 26,696 − 28,344 + 184 (other operating income) = -1,464. Other operating income explains the difference between gross profit less expenses and operating loss.
- Cash composition: 53,817 cash and cash equivalents + 252,214 short-term bank deposits + 20,000 short-term bank financial products + 37,000 long-term bank deposits = 363,031. This excludes restricted cash and separately listed short- and long-term investments.
- Free cash flow: 2,153 − 101 = 2,052. The table's capex and FCF definition excludes separately reported headquarters construction purchases of 3,037. Including those purchases would produce -985 (US$-0.985 million); that alternative is not substituted into the existing series.

## Operating values

- Combined dollar-based net retention rate: **110%**.
- Active customers: **4,024**, versus **3,944** as of September 30, 2025. These are customer account counts, with an active customer defined as an organization or individual developer generating more than US$100 in the preceding 12 months, excluding Easemob customers.
- Agora and Shengwang revenue and retention rates are not separately disclosed in this release. The corresponding table cells remain `null`, rendered blank; no divisional estimates are introduced.

The release reports 97% as the comparable combined Q3 2025 retention rate. The historical table has divisional retention rates of 108% and 90% for that quarter; these are different scopes, so neither is replaced with the new release's combined comparator.
